Brief History

The history of Palestine is complex and multifaceted. Over the centuries, this region has seen various civilizations and conflicts.

In the 20th century, the establishment of the state of Israel in 1948 led to significant changes, including the displacement of many Palestinians.

Despite ongoing challenges, the people of Palestine continue to strive for peace and recognition on the world stage.
