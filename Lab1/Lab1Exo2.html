Since October 7, Gaza has witnessed one of the harshest wars in its history, resulting in the deaths of over 64,000 people, including a significant number of children and women.

In addition to human losses, the infrastructure in Gaza has been severely destroyed. Approximately 210,000 housing units have been obliterated, leaving thousands homeless.

Health and educational institutions have also faced extensive destruction, with 156 schools and universities being destroyed.

The war has inflicted significant damage. We all must work to end this suffering.
